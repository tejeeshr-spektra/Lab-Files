Zava Retail — Returns & Product Availability FAQ
==================================================

RETURNS POLICY
---------------
- Zava Retail accepts returns within 60 days of purchase with a valid receipt or proof of purchase.
- Items must be unworn/unused, in original packaging, and with tags attached.
- Hiking boots and other footwear are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged once worn outdoors.
- Clearance and marked-down items are final sale.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of the return being received.
- Gift returns without a receipt will be issued store credit at the current selling price.

STORE HOURS
------------
- Monday–Friday: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- Hours may vary on public holidays; check the local store listing.

PRODUCT AVAILABILITY
----------------------
- Tents, backpacks, and sleeping bags: generally in stock year-round, with seasonal colorways rotating each spring and fall.
- Hiking boots and trail shoes: sized S–XXL, wide-width options available in select styles.
- Winter apparel (jackets, base layers): stock is highest September–February; limited availability in summer months.
- Out-of-stock items can typically be ordered online for home delivery or in-store pickup within 3–5 business days.

SHIPPING
---------
- Standard shipping: 3–5 business days.
- Express shipping: 1–2 business days (additional fee applies).
- Free standard shipping on orders over $75.
